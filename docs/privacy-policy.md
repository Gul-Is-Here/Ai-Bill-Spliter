# Privacy Policy — AI Bill Splitter

Last updated: November 2026

AI Bill Splitter ("the app") helps you scan receipts and split bills. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

1. Data you provide
• Account: your name, email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your Google profile photo. Used to sign you in and keep your data under your account.
• Bills and groups: items, amounts, currencies, dates, and the names of people you add. Stored in your private account space on Google Firebase.
• Receipt photos: uploaded to your private Firebase Storage folder and sent to Google's Gemini API through our server to read the items.
• Voice: when you use voice split, speech is converted to text by your device's speech service; the text is sent to our server and Google's Gemini API to understand the split. Audio is not stored by the app.
• Contacts: when you pick a contact, only the chosen name is read and added to the bill. Your contact list is never uploaded.

2. Data collected automatically
• Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): app events such as scans and splits, app version and device type.
• Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): crash logs and device state to fix bugs.

3. How data is shared
We do not sell your data. Data is processed by Google (Firebase, Gemini API) as our service provider. Other people only see a bill if you share it yourself.

4. Retention and deletion
Data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a bill removes it and its receipt. Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account, bills, groups and receipt photos from our servers.

5. Security
Data is sent over encrypted connections, and security rules ensure only your account can access your data.

6. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

7. Contact
Questions or requests: support@aibillsplitter.app
