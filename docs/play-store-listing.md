# Play Store listing draft

**App name (30 max):** AI Bill Splitter: Scan & Split

**Short description (80 max):**
Scan a receipt, split it by item or equally, and settle up with friends.

**Full description:**

Eating out with friends, sharing a flat, or on a trip? AI Bill Splitter reads your receipt and works out who owes what, to the last paisa.

📸 Scan any receipt
Take a photo and AI picks out every item, tax, service charge, tip and discount. You can review and edit everything before splitting.

➗ Split your way
• Equally
• By item: tap who had what; shared items are split automatically
• By percentage
Tax, tips and discounts are shared in proportion to what each person ordered, and the totals always add up exactly.

🎙️ Split by voice
Just say "Ali had the burger, Sara and I shared the pizza, Sara paid" in English or Urdu.

👥 Groups & trips
Keep bills for a trip, a flat or a friend group in one place. See who owes whom and get the fewest payments needed to settle up.

📤 Share instantly
Send a clear breakdown to WhatsApp or any app.

🌐 Works offline
Bills are saved on your phone and sync when you're back online.

🇵🇰 English & اردو
Full Urdu support with right-to-left layout. PKR by default, with other currencies supported.

🔒 Private by design
Your bills are only visible to you. Delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

Free, with no ads.
